Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This policy explains what Agentic GUARDIANS (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website or join our early access list, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Agentic GUARDIANS, 1521 Concord Pike, Suite 301, Wilmington, DE 19803, USA. You can reach us about privacy at privacy@agenticguardians.com.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your work email address when you sign up. If you choose to, what describes you (for example “Using agents”) and what you run. Anything you write to us by email.
  • What your browser sends. Like any website, our host receives your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in server logs.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site. The site stores one small value in your browser’s local storage to vary the example text on phones. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about early access to Agentic GUARDIANS and about product updates.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, our legal basis is your consent when you sign up, and our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We use service providers to host this site, store the sign-up list and send email. They may process your information only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Where it is processed

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can unsubscribe from any email we send using the link in it. Depending on where you live (including the EEA, the UK and California), you may have additional rights, such as objecting to processing or lodging a complaint with your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email privacy@agenticguardians.com. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the changes are significant, we will tell people on our list by email.

Contact

Questions: privacy@agenticguardians.com.
