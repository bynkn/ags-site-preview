Trust & proof

Trust is earned. Here’s how we earn it.

A guard you can’t check is just another thing to trust. So here is what we touch, what we don’t, what we can prove, and where our limits are.

What we touch

Small footprint. On purpose.

Where it runs
On your machine. Nothing leaves it.
What it reads
Which tool, in what order. Not prompts, messages or files.
What it runs on
A lightweight CPU. No GPU. No tokens.
If it’s down
The action doesn’t run.
What it keeps
A signed record of holds and blocks.

The record

Signed. Chained. Checkable.

Blocks and holds can carry a signed record (Ed25519), chained so any tampering shows. Verify it with our public key.

Each record is signed with our private key, and anyone can check it with our public key. Each record also links to the one before it, so a changed or deleted record shows.

What a record shows

The agent, the tool, the decision and the reason. And that no record in the chain was changed or removed.

What it doesn’t show

Actions that never went through a connected platform. Or that a decision was the right one. That’s what review is for.

Coverage

Honest about scope.

We see the actions that go through a connected agent platform. One engine, one small connector per platform. Actions outside a connected platform aren’t covered, and we won’t pretend they are.

If we can’t check an action, it doesn’t run.

Questions

What security teams ask.

What do you read?

Which tool the agent calls, and in what order. We don’t read or store prompts, messages or files.

Where does the data go?

Nowhere. It runs on your machine. Nothing leaves it.

What happens if the engine is down?

The action doesn’t run. If we can’t check an action, it doesn’t run.

Is there an AI model in the decision?

No. The decision is math on the agent’s behavior. No AI models in the loop, no GPU and no tokens.

How do I check a record?

Blocks and holds can carry a signed record (Ed25519), chained so any tampering shows. Verify it with our public key.

What happens when it’s wrong?

Holds go to a person. Any block can be reviewed and marked right or wrong, and that agent’s baseline learns from it.

The weight

No AI. No GPU. No tokens.

  • No AI models in the loop. The decision is math on behavior. There is nothing to prompt and nothing to talk around.
  • Runs on a lightweight CPU, on your machine. Nothing leaves it.
  • No GPU bill. No token bill. Checking an action doesn’t mean paying for another model to think.
